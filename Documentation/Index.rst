ViewMend Site Tracker

ViewMend is a website quality and operations platform. Site Tracker is its
module for confirmed website change and deployment monitoring. This official
TYPO3 extension stores operational events locally and delivers them
asynchronously through the published ViewMend SDK.

The extension is designed for TYPO3 13.4 and 14.3 on PHP 8.3 through 8.5.
